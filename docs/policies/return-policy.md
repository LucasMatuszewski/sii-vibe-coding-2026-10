# Return Policy (example company document)

Version: 1.0 (example, 2026-10-18). Status: sample policy for the MVP, to be replaced by the Company's approved document.

This document is injected into the decision agent's instructions for the **return** scenario. The agent must apply only the rules below and cite the section number (for example "R-3.2") in its justification.

---

## R-1. Scope

R-1.1. A return means the customer withdraws from the purchase without giving a reason and expects a refund. A return is not a complaint about a defect; defects are handled under the Complaint Policy.

R-1.2. For distance purchases (online shop, phone order), the customer has a statutory right to withdraw within 14 days of taking possession of the product (Polish Consumer Rights Act). The Company voluntarily extends the same 14-day return window to in-store purchases.

R-1.3. The return window is counted from the date of purchase entered by the customer. If the customer states that the product was delivered later than the purchase date, the delivery date counts, subject to verification by staff.

## R-2. Return window

R-2.1. Day 0 to day 14 after the purchase date: the return window is open.

R-2.2. Day 15 to day 21 after the purchase date: the window may still be open if delivery was later than purchase. The agent returns **Needs information** and asks for the delivery date.

R-2.3. More than 21 days after the purchase date: the return window is closed. The agent returns **Rejected**, unless the customer describes a defect, in which case the agent directs the customer to file a complaint instead.

## R-3. Condition of the product

The product must be resellable. Handling the product to check its nature, features and functioning (as in a physical store) is allowed.

R-3.1. **Acceptable, return accepted:** no visible damage; no signs of use beyond unpacking and testing; protective films removed; minor dust or fingerprints.

R-3.2. **Acceptable with possible value deduction:** light handling marks that do not affect function (for example a faint micro-scratch on a casing, minor wear on packaging). The return is accepted; the customer is informed that after inspection the refund may be reduced for diminished value caused by handling beyond what was necessary.

R-3.3. **Not acceptable as a standard return:** visible damage or clear signs of regular use, including cracked or scratched screens, dents, chipped corners, stains, liquid marks, missing parts, engravings, stickers that cannot be removed, or heavy wear. The agent returns **Rejected** for the standard return and explains that the customer may contact customer support about a return with a value deduction, or file a complaint if the damage is a defect not caused by the customer.

R-3.4. Missing original packaging is not a ground for rejection on its own. The customer is informed that a value deduction may apply after inspection.

## R-4. Excluded products

R-4.1. In-ear headphones and other products delivered in a sealed package that cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, if the seal was broken.

R-4.2. Software, games and licences delivered in a sealed package, if the seal was broken.

R-4.3. Products made to the customer's specification (for example a custom-configured laptop).

For an excluded product, the agent returns **Rejected** and cites the exclusion. If the photo does not show whether the seal is intact, the agent returns **Needs information**.

## R-5. Evidence requirements

R-5.1. The photo must show the whole device or its main surface (for screens: the screen side) clearly enough to judge its condition.

R-5.2. If the photo is blurry, too dark, shows a different device than declared, or does not show the device at all, the agent returns **Needs information** and describes the photo the customer should take in a new case.

## R-6. Refund and next steps (for accepted returns)

R-6.1. The customer sends or brings the product back within 14 days of submitting the return, with all accessories received.

R-6.2. The Company refunds the payment within 14 days of receiving the withdrawal statement, but may withhold the refund until the product is received back.

R-6.3. The final refund amount is confirmed only after physical inspection.
